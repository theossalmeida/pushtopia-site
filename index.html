The loop

A simple rhythm for your day.

Pushtopia connects to the GitHub account you choose inside the macOS app. It quietly polls your activity and lets your rhythm shape the world — no backend, no webhook, nothing installed in your repos.

  1. 01

    Sign in on your Mac

    GitHub OAuth Device Flow, inside the app. Your token lives in your Keychain. Read-only scopes.

    $ open -a Pushtopia # → enter code ABCD-1234
  2. 02

    Pick your repos

    Choose which repositories count. Pushtopia only watches what you tell it to.

    watching acme/api · acme/web · you/dotfiles
  3. 03

    Keep building

    A single GraphQL heartbeat checks pushes, workflow runs and reviews every few seconds.

    $ git push # → satiety +10, Brita hops
  4. 04

    Care for your pet

    Check in from the menu bar, notice its state, and let your coding rhythm shape the world.

    status ◆ 70 · ♥ 80 · ☼ 60

Privacy by default

Your code stays yours.

This page never touches your GitHub. Sign-in happens in the macOS app through GitHub's OAuth Device Flow. No server of ours, no webhooks, no repo hooks — the app reads events, counts them, and forgets them.

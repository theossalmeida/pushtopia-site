The loop

A simple rhythm for your day.

Pushtopia connects to the GitHub account you choose inside the macOS app. Your activity helps your pet stay well and unlocks gentle changes over time.

  1. 01

    Sign in on your Mac

    The MVP uses GitHub OAuth App Device Flow inside the app.

  2. 02

    Keep building

    Commits and other supported activity become signals for your companion.

  3. 03

    Care for your pet

    Check in, notice its state, and let your coding rhythm shape the world.

Privacy by default

Your code stays yours.
